An encrypted vault for every client's API keys

Key Vault is a downloadable browser extension for developers and freelancers. It keeps your keys and client details encrypted on your own device and opens in the side panel while you work.

Everything for managing client credentials

One place for the keys you use every day, organized the way you actually work.

Encrypted on your device

Your vault is encrypted with a master password that only you know. The developer cannot read it.

Quick PIN unlock

Open the vault with a 4-digit PIN. It locks automatically when you are idle or your screen locks.

A workspace per client

Create each client once, then keep its keys, notes, contact details and custom fields together.

Copy safely

One-tap copy, with the clipboard cleared automatically after 30 seconds. Keys stay hidden until you reveal them.

Side panel

Stays open next to your work, so you never click a popup open and shut again.

Sync and backups

Optionally sync the encrypted vault through your browser profile, and export encrypted backups.

How it works

Getting the paid plan is fully automatic. Nothing is delivered by hand.

  1. Install the extension. Get Key Vault from the Microsoft Edge Add-ons store, create your master password and set your 4-digit PIN.
  2. Upgrade when you need more clients. Click Subscribe in the extension to open the secure checkout.
  3. Receive your license key by email. It is generated and sent automatically right after payment.
  4. Paste the key into the extension. Open Settings, choose Upgrade, paste the key and press Activate. One key works on up to 3 devices.
  5. Cancel any time. If your subscription ends, the extension returns to the free plan and your saved keys stay readable and exportable.
