Key Vault is a Chrome browser extension that opens in the side panel and lets freelancers and developers keep API keys and credentials in an encrypted vault stored on their own device. Keys are organized into Personal and per-client workspaces.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Key Vault and digital licensing.

What is Key Vault?

Key Vault is a Chrome browser extension that opens in the side panel and lets freelancers and developers keep API keys and credentials in an encrypted vault stored on their own device. Keys are organized into Personal and per-client workspaces.

How does the subscription and activation work?

The extension is free for personal use, and a monthly or yearly subscription unlocks unlimited client workspaces. After paying, the customer receives a license key by email and pastes it into the extension to activate it. No physical goods or services are involved.

Where are my API keys stored?

Keys are encrypted locally and stored on your own device using Chrome's secure storage APIs. No passwords, credentials, or keys are ever transmitted to or stored on Genstay servers.

Who develops and publishes Key Vault?

Key Vault is developed and published by Genstay, founded by Praveen Reddy. Support inquiries can be directed to genstay.in@gmail.com.
