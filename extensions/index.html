How to install

Personal Browser OS is preparing for launch on Microsoft Edge Add-ons. It will take under a minute to install.

Open Microsoft Edge Add-ons

Visit the Microsoft Edge Add-ons store in your Edge browser, or click the Get button on this page once we launch.

Search for “Personal Browser OS”

Find the extension by name — category: Productivity. Look for the calm green icon.

Click Get, then Add extension

Confirm the permissions. Personal Browser OS explains each permission in plain language on its store listing.

Open a new tab

Your calm new tab appears instantly. Open the pet menu to choose your companion, and the options page to tune themes, fonts and (optionally) AI chat.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you might want to know before installing.

What is Personal Browser OS?

Personal Browser OS is a free browser extension for Microsoft Edge and Google Chrome that replaces your new tab with a calm, customisable page, cleans up Google search results, and gives you a 3D pet companion you can name, talk to and chat with. It also adds a side-panel Library for history and tabs, plus themes, fonts and optional bring-your-own-key AI chat.

Is Personal Browser OS free?

Yes, completely free. No ads, no subscriptions, no hidden charges. If you enable the AI chat, you bring your own API key from OpenAI, Gemini or OpenRouter and pay those providers directly — the extension itself costs nothing.

Does it work on Chrome or only Edge?

Personal Browser OS is built on Manifest V3 and runs on any Chromium-based browser, including Microsoft Edge and Google Chrome. It launches first on Microsoft Edge Add-ons; Chrome support uses the same package.

Does the extension collect or sell my data?

No. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Your settings, notes, backgrounds and pet customisation are stored locally in your browser. Your AI API keys stay on your device and are only sent to the AI provider you chose. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

What is the 3D pet and can I add my own?

The pet is a small animated companion that lives on your pages — choose a cat, fox, bunny, panda or a full 3D pet, give it a name, talk to it by voice, or chat with it. You can also upload your own image from the pet menu to use as your companion.

How does the AI chat work?

The AI chat is optional and bring-your-own-key. Paste an API key from OpenAI, Google Gemini or OpenRouter in the extension settings, pick your model, and chat with your pet. The key is stored locally on your device and requests go only to the provider you configured.

Why does the extension ask for these permissions?

Storage keeps your settings and backgrounds. History, tabs and sessions power the side-panel Library so you can find open, recent and closed tabs. The sidePanel permission hosts that Library. Favicon shows site icons in the Library. Host permissions are used only for optional features: AI chat endpoints if you add a key, and IP-based weather and location for the new tab page. Nothing is used to track you.
